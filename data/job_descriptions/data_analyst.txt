JOB DESCRIPTION — SAMPLE / PLACEHOLDER (for Phase 6 Recruitment Agent testing)

Title: Data Analyst
Department: People Analytics
Location: Pune, India (Hybrid)
Experience: 1-3 years

About the Role
We are looking for a Data Analyst to support workforce reporting — turning
engagement, performance, and attrition data into dashboards and insights
that HR and business leaders act on.

Required Skills
- SQL (intermediate to advanced)
- Excel (pivot tables, formulas, basic macros)
- Data visualization tool: Power BI or Tableau
- Python or R for ad-hoc analysis (pandas preferred)
- Strong written communication for presenting findings to non-technical
  stakeholders

Good to Have
- Experience with HR/people analytics specifically
- Basic statistics (hypothesis testing, correlation vs causation)
- Exposure to Streamlit or similar for lightweight internal dashboards

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain recurring dashboards (attrition, engagement, training
  completion) for HR leadership
- Investigate ad-hoc questions from business stakeholders and turn them
  into clear, defensible analysis
- Partner with the ML team to validate that model outputs (e.g. attrition
  risk scores) align with what analysts see in the raw data
- Maintain data quality checks on core HR tables

Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in any quantitative field
- Comfortable working with messy, real-world HR data
